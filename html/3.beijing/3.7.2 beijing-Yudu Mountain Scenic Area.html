Yudu Mountain Scenic Area Overview

A comprehensive guide to Yudu Mountain Scenic Area, a beautiful mountain area with hiking trails.

Yudu Mountain Scenic Area is a beautiful mountain area located in Yanqing District, Beijing. Known for its stunning natural landscapes, diverse hiking trails, and fresh mountain air, it's a popular destination for outdoor enthusiasts.

The area features mountain peaks, valleys, waterfalls, and forests, offering visitors a chance to escape the city and enjoy nature. It's particularly beautiful during spring when flowers bloom and autumn when leaves change color.

The scenic area includes famous Yudu Mountain Peak, Waterfall Valley, and various other natural attractions. It's a perfect place for hiking, camping, and enjoying the outdoors.

Recommended Tour Route

Yudu Mountain Scenic Area is best explored by following a route that covers main trails and viewpoints. Start at the main entrance and proceed through the mountain area.

Key attractions include the Yudu Mountain Peak, Waterfall Valley, and various hiking trails offering different levels of difficulty.

The area is particularly beautiful during spring when wildflowers are in bloom and autumn when leaves change color. Don't miss the opportunity to hike to the summit for panoramic views of the surrounding landscape.
