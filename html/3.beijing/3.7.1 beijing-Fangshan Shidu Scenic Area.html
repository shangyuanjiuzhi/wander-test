Fangshan Shidu Scenic Area Overview

A comprehensive guide to Fangshan Shidu Scenic Area, a beautiful karst landscape with outdoor activities.

Fangshan Shidu Scenic Area is a beautiful karst landscape located in Fangshan District, Beijing. Known for its unique geological formations, clear rivers, and outdoor activities, it's a popular destination for nature lovers and adventure seekers.

The area is famous for its ten crossings (Shidu in Chinese) of the Juma River, creating a series of scenic gorges and valleys. It offers various outdoor activities including rafting, hiking, and rock climbing.

The scenic area includes famous Ten Crossings of Juma River, Karst Caves, and various other natural attractions. It's a perfect place for outdoor adventures and enjoying nature.

Recommended Tour Route

Fangshan Shidu Scenic Area is best explored by following a route that covers main attractions and outdoor activities. Start at the main entrance and proceed through the scenic area.

Key attractions include the Ten Crossings of Juma River, Karst Caves, and various outdoor activities such as rafting and hiking.

The area is particularly beautiful during spring and autumn when the weather is pleasant for outdoor activities. Don't miss the opportunity to experience rafting on the Juma River and explore the unique karst formations.
