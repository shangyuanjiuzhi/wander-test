Beijing Garden Expo Park Overview

A comprehensive guide to Beijing Garden Expo Park, featuring various themed gardens from around the world.

Beijing Garden Expo Park is a large park located in the Fengtai District of Beijing. It was built for the 9th China (Beijing) International Garden Expo in 2013 and covers an area of 513 hectares.

The park features various themed gardens from around the world, showcasing different gardening styles and techniques. It includes gardens from China, Japan, Europe, and other regions, as well as specialized gardens such as the Rose Garden, Peony Garden, and Bamboo Garden. It features a total of 69 gardens.

Beijing Garden Expo Park is a popular spot for sightseeing, photography, and outdoor activities. It's also a great place to learn about different gardening traditions from around the world.

Recommended Tour Route

Beijing Garden Expo Park is best explored by following a route that takes you through the main themed gardens. Start at the Main Gate, which is the entrance to the park.

From the Main Gate, visit the Chinese Garden, which features traditional Chinese gardening styles. Then explore the International Garden, which showcases gardens from different countries around the world.

Continue to the Specialized Gardens, including the Rose Garden, Peony Garden, and Bamboo Garden. Along the way, you'll enjoy beautiful views of the park's lakes and landscaping.

Detailed Information

Location

Beijing Garden Expo Park, Fengtai District, Beijing, China

Opening Hours
  • Peak Season (Mar 15 - Oct 31): 6:30 - 19:00
  • Off Season (Nov 1 - Mar 16): 7:30 - 18:00
Tickets
  • Adults: ¥20 per person
Reservation Information
  • Tickets can be purchased at the entrance on the day of visit.
  • No advance booking is required, but it's recommended to arrive early to avoid crowds.
Transportation
  • Metro: Take Line 14 to Garden Expo Park Station (Exit A)
  • Taxi: Directly to Beijing Garden Expo Park Main Gate
