Capital Museum Overview

A comprehensive guide to the Capital Museum, showcasing Beijing's history and culture through a diverse collection of artifacts.

The Capital Museum is located in the heart of Beijing, in the Xicheng District. It showcases the history and culture of the city through a diverse collection of artifacts, including ancient pottery, jade, bronze ware, calligraphy, and paintings.

The museum's collection spans over 3,000 years of Beijing's history, from prehistoric times to the present day. It features permanent exhibitions such as Ancient Beijing, Folk Customs of Old Beijing, and various special exhibitions that rotate throughout the year.

The museum is known for its modern architecture and interactive displays, making it a popular destination for both locals and tourists interested in learning about Beijing's rich cultural heritage.

Recommended Tour Route

The Capital Museum is best explored by starting from the Ancient Beijing exhibition on the first floor, which provides a comprehensive overview of Beijing's history from prehistoric times to the Qing Dynasty.

After exploring the Ancient Beijing exhibition, visit the Folk Customs of Old Beijing exhibition, which showcases the traditional customs and lifestyle of Beijing residents through various artifacts and interactive displays.

Don't miss the various special exhibitions that rotate throughout the year, showcasing different aspects of Beijing's culture and art. The museum also features a gift shop and a café for visitors to relax and purchase souvenirs.
