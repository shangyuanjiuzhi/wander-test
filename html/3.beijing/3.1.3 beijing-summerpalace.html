Summer Palace Overview

A comprehensive guide to the Summer Palace, one of China's most beautiful imperial gardens.

The Summer Palace, located in the western suburbs of Beijing, is a vast imperial garden complex and a UNESCO World Heritage site. Originally built in the 18th century during the Qing Dynasty, it served as a summer retreat for emperors and their families to escape the heat of the capital.

Covering an area of 290 hectares, including a 70-hectare lake, the Summer Palace is renowned for its harmonious blend of natural scenery and traditional Chinese architecture. It features numerous pavilions, halls, bridges, and gardens, all arranged around Kunming Lake and Longevity Hill.

The Summer Palace is often praised as the epitome of Chinese garden design, showcasing the philosophy of "borrowing scenery" (jiing) where the natural landscape is integrated with man-made structures to create a seamless and picturesque environment.

Recommended Tour Route

The Summer Palace is best explored by following a route that covers the main scenic areas. Start at the East Gate and proceed to the Hall of Benevolence and Longevity (Renshoudian), the main administrative building where the emperor handled state affairs.

From there, visit the Hall of Jade Ripples (Yulantang), the emperor's living quarters, and the Long Corridor (Changlang), a 728-meter covered walkway with over 14,000 painted scenes.

Continue to the Marble Boat (Shifang) on the western shore, then climb Longevity Hill to reach the Tower of Buddhist Incense (Foxiangge), the highest point in the garden offering panoramic views of the entire complex.

Finally, enjoy a boat ride on Kunming Lake to experience the garden from a different perspective, and visit the Seventeen-Arch Bridge (Shiqikong Qiao) connecting the eastern shore to Nanhu Island(South Lake Island).

Detailed Information

Location

Summer Palace, Haidian District, Beijing, China

Opening Hours
  • Peak Season（April 1 - October 31）:
    6:00 - 20:00
  • Off-Season（November 1 - March 31）:
    6:30 - 19:00
Tickets
  • Peak Season: Summer Palace ticket 30 CNY / Summer Palace combo ticket 60 CNY
  • Off Season: Summer Palace ticket 20 CNY / Summer Palace combo ticket 50 CNY
  • Inside Gardens: Dehe Garden 5 CNY; Summer Palace Museum 20 CNY; Foxiang Pavilion 10 CNY; Suzhou Street 10 CNY
  • Note: The combo ticket includes the entrance ticket and tickets to the inside gardens.
Reservation Information
  • Book tickets online through the official website or search for "颐和园"(Summer Palace) in WeChat mini program（Only available in Chinese）
  • Tickets can also be purchased at the entrance, but advance booking is recommended to avoid long queues.
Transportation
  • To West Gate: Take bus 469 or 539 to Summer Palace West Gate Station, then walk to the gate
  • To North Gate: Take Metro Line 4 to North Gate Station (Exit D), or take bus 303, 332, 346, 384, 394, or 563 to Summer Palace North Gate Station, then walk to the gate
  • To East Gate: Take Metro Line 4 to Xiyuan Station (Exit C1/C2), or take bus 303, 332, 346, 584, or Night 8 to Summer Palace Station, then walk to the gate
  • To XinJian Palace Gate: Take bus 374, 437, or 74 to Summer Palace New Palace Gate（XinJian Palace Gate） Station, then walk to the gate
Audio Guide Rental
  • Location: Available at the East Gate, North Gate, and West Gate visitor centers
  • Price: ¥20 per device (includes earphones)
  • Languages: Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, French, German, Spanish, Russian
  • Deposit: ¥100 or a valid ID is required
