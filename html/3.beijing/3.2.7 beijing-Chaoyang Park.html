Chaoyang Park Overview

A comprehensive guide to Chaoyang Park, one of the largest parks in Beijing featuring lakes, hills, and various recreational facilities.

Chaoyang Park is one of the largest parks in Beijing, located in the Chaoyang District. It covers an area of 288 hectares and features lakes, hills, and various recreational facilities.

Chaoyang Park was founded in November 1984. The park is a popular spot for jogging, cycling, and outdoor activities, especially on weekends. It features a large lake where visitors can rent boats, as well as playgrounds, sports facilities, and picnic areas.

Chaoyang Park is also known for its beautiful landscaping, with numerous trees, flowers, and scenic spots. It's a great place to escape the hustle and bustle of the city and enjoy some peace and quiet in nature.

Recommended Tour Route

Chaoyang Park is best explored by following a route that takes you through the main scenic areas and recreational facilities. Start at the West Gate, which is the main entrance to the park.

From the West Gate, follow the path to the Central Lake, where you can rent a boat and enjoy the scenery. Then head to the East Lake area, which features playgrounds and sports facilities.

Continue to the Northern Scenic Area, which features hills and forests, offering a peaceful retreat from the city. Along the way, you'll pass several pavilions and scenic viewpoints.
