Red Brick Art Museum Overview

A comprehensive guide to the Red Brick Art Museum, Beijing's distinctive contemporary art venue.

The Red Brick Art Museum is a contemporary art museum located in the Chaoyang District of Beijing. Designed by Chinese architect Dong Yugan, it opened in 2014 and is known for its distinctive red brick architecture.

The museum's design incorporates traditional Chinese architectural elements with modern sensibilities, creating a unique space for contemporary art exhibitions. The red brick exterior and interior spaces provide a warm, textured backdrop for the artworks on display.

The museum features rotating exhibitions of contemporary art from both Chinese and international artists. It also includes a beautiful garden space with sculptures and outdoor installations, making it a popular destination for art lovers and photographers.

Recommended Tour Route

The Red Brick Art Museum is best explored by starting at the main entrance and following a path through the various exhibition halls. The museum's layout is designed to guide visitors through a sequence of spaces, each offering a different viewing experience.

Key areas to visit include the main exhibition halls, which feature rotating contemporary art exhibitions, and the outdoor garden space, which includes sculptures and installations. The museum's red brick architecture itself is also a highlight, with its distinctive design and use of natural light.

Don't miss the opportunity to explore the museum's café and shop, which offer a selection of art-related books, gifts, and refreshments. The museum's garden is also a great place to relax and take in the surrounding scenery.
