SOLANA Overview

A comprehensive guide to SOLANA, a modern shopping and entertainment complex with a European-style waterfront design.

SOLANA is a modern shopping and entertainment complex located in the Chaoyang District of Beijing. With its European-style waterfront design, it offers a unique shopping and dining experience in a scenic setting.

The complex features a wide range of international brands, restaurants, cafes, and entertainment options. It's known for its beautiful architecture, waterfront promenade, and vibrant atmosphere, especially in the evening when the area is illuminated with colorful lights.

SOLANA is not just a shopping destination but also a popular spot for socializing, dining, and entertainment. It hosts various events and activities throughout the year, making it a dynamic and lively place to visit.

Recommended Tour Route

SOLANA is best explored by starting from the main entrance and wandering through the waterfront promenade, exploring the various shops and restaurants along the way.

Key attractions include the SOLANA Shopping Center, featuring international brands and local boutiques, the Waterfront Dining Area with a variety of restaurants offering different cuisines, and the Central Square, which often hosts events and performances.

Don't miss the SOLANA Cinema for movie screenings, the Children's Play Area for families, and the beautiful waterfront views, especially during sunset and evening when the area is beautifully lit.
