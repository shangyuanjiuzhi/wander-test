National Botanical Garden Overview

A comprehensive guide to the National Botanical Garden, a vast botanical garden featuring a wide variety of plants from around the world.

The National Botanical Garden is a vast botanical garden located in the Haidian District of Beijing. It features a wide variety of plants from around the world, including greenhouses, gardens, and walking trails.

The garden covers an area of 400 hectares and is home to over 10,000 species of plants. It includes several specialized gardens, such as the Rose Garden, Peony Garden, and Bamboo Garden, as well as greenhouses that house tropical and subtropical plants.

The National Botanical Garden is a perfect place for nature lovers and families, offering a peaceful retreat from the city. It's also an important research and conservation center for plants in China.

Recommended Tour Route

The National Botanical Garden is best explored by following a route that takes you through the main gardens and greenhouses. Start at the East Gate, which is the main entrance to the park.

From the East Gate, visit the Rose Garden, Peony Garden, and Bamboo Garden. Then head to the Conservatory, which houses tropical and subtropical plants.

Continue to the Alpine Garden, which features plants from mountainous regions, and the Medicinal Plant Garden, which displays plants used in traditional Chinese medicine.

The garden is quite large, so plan to spend at least 3-4 hours to fully explore its attractions.
