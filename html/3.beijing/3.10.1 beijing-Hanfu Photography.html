Experience traditional Chinese clothing and capture stunning photos at Beijing's historic landmarks

About Hanfu Photography

Hanfu is the traditional clothing of the Han Chinese people, with a history dating back thousands of years. In recent years, Hanfu photography has become increasingly popular among both locals and tourists in Beijing.

Wearing traditional Hanfu while visiting Beijing's historic sites allows you to immerse yourself in the city's rich cultural heritage and create unique, timeless photographs that blend history with modern aesthetics.

Many photography studios in Beijing now offer complete Hanfu photography packages, including costume rental, professional makeup, and guided photo sessions at iconic locations.
