Chao Shang Chao (潮上潮)

Michelin 3 Star

Introduction

Chao Shang Chao is a Michelin 3-star restaurant specializing in premium Cantonese cuisine. Located in the Zhengda building, it offers an exceptional dining experience with the finest seafood, bird's nest, and other luxury ingredients prepared with impeccable technique. The restaurant is renowned for its elegant ambiance and impeccable service.

Recommended Dishes

1. 脆皮炸乳鸽皇
2. 吊炉烧饼配隆江猪手（半打）
3. 潮式冻午笋饭
4. 佛手柑

Price Range

Average cost per person: ¥800-2000

Address

Zhengda Building, Gongtibeili, Chaoyang District, Beijing

Xin Rong Ji (新荣记)

Michelin 3 Star

Introduction

Xin Rong Ji is a Michelin 3-star restaurant specializing in Zhejiang cuisine with a focus on fresh seafood. The restaurant is known for its high-quality ingredients and authentic flavors, offering a refined dining experience that highlights the natural taste of ingredients. Its elegant ambiance and impeccable service make it a top choice for business dinners and special occasions.

Recommended Dishes

1. 脆皮妙龄鸽
2. 黄金脆带鱼
3. 沙蒜烧豆面
4. 家烧东海大黄鱼

Price Range

Average cost per person: ¥500-1000

Address

No. 8 Dongzhimen Wai Street, Chaoyang District, Beijing

Jing Ji (京季荣派官府菜)

Michelin 2 Star

Introduction

Jing Ji is a Michelin 2-star restaurant offering creative Cantonese cuisine with a modern twist. The restaurant combines traditional Cantonese cooking techniques with innovative presentations, creating dishes that are both visually stunning and delicious. Located in a sophisticated setting, it provides an exceptional dining experience for discerning food lovers.

Recommended Dishes

1. 官府黄焖佛跳墙
2. 咖喱汁脆皮牛腩
3. 北方虾酱蒸青岛
4. 老北京烧油皮带鱼

Price Range

Average cost per person: ¥400-800

Address

Pacific Place Building, Jingguan Tower, Chaoyang District, Beijing

King Joy (京兆尹)

Michelin 2 Star

Introduction

King Joy is a Michelin 2-star vegetarian restaurant located in a traditional courtyard house near the Forbidden City. It offers creative vegetarian cuisine that combines traditional Chinese flavors with modern presentation. The restaurant's tranquil atmosphere and healthy, artistic dishes make it a unique dining destination for those seeking a refined vegetarian experience.

Recommended Dishes

1. 桃胶珍菌卤味饭
2. 糖醋藕排
3. 金刚沙豆腐
4. 松茸杂菌煎

Price Range

Average cost per person: ¥600-1200

Address

No. 23 Donghuamen Street, Dongcheng District, Beijing
