CCTV Tower Overview

A comprehensive guide to the CCTV Tower, Beijing's iconic telecommunications and observation tower.

The CCTV Tower is a telecommunications and observation tower located in the Haidian District of Beijing. Completed in 1992, it stands at 386.5 meters tall, making it one of the tallest structures in the city.

The tower features an observation deck at 238 meters, offering panoramic views of Beijing. It also houses a revolving restaurant, a café, and a gift shop. The tower's distinctive design, with its cylindrical shape and antenna, has made it a recognizable landmark in Beijing's skyline.

The CCTV Tower is not only a functional telecommunications facility but also a popular tourist attraction. Visitors can take the elevator to the observation deck to enjoy breathtaking views of the city, especially during sunset and at night when the city is illuminated.

Recommended Visit

The CCTV Tower is best explored by taking the elevator to the observation deck, where you can enjoy panoramic views of Beijing. The observation deck offers 360-degree views of the city, with informative displays about the landmarks visible in each direction.

After visiting the observation deck, you can dine at the revolving restaurant, which offers both Chinese and Western cuisine while providing changing views of the city. The tower also has a café and gift shop where you can purchase souvenirs.

For the best experience, visit the CCTV Tower during sunset or at night when the city is illuminated. The observation deck provides a stunning view of Beijing's skyline, including the nearby Summer Palace and other landmarks.
