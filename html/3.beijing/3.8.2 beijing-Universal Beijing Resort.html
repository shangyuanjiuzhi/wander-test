Universal Beijing Resort Overview

A comprehensive guide to Universal Beijing Resort, a world-class theme park.

Universal Beijing Resort is a world-class theme park located in Tongzhou District, Beijing. Opened in 2021, it covers an area of 400 hectares and features Universal Studios Beijing, Universal CityWalk, and two hotels.

The resort is known for its thrilling rides, immersive experiences, and entertainment based on popular Universal films. It's divided into several themed zones, each offering unique experiences for visitors of all ages.

The resort includes famous Universal Studios Beijing, Universal CityWalk, and various other themed areas. It's a perfect place for families, movie fans, and anyone looking for a day of fun.

Recommended Tour Route

Universal Beijing Resort is best explored by following a route that covers main attractions and rides. Start at the main entrance and proceed through the park.

Key attractions include the Universal Studios Beijing, Universal CityWalk, and various themed zones offering different experiences.

The resort offers rides for all ages, from gentle carousels for children to thrilling roller coasters for adults. Don't miss the opportunity to experience the park's signature rides and shows.
