About Beijing

A City of Contrasts

Beijing, the capital of China, is a city where ancient history and modern innovation coexist harmoniously. With a history spanning over 3,000 years, it served as the imperial capital for five dynasties, leaving behind a wealth of cultural heritage.

Today, Beijing is a bustling metropolis with skyscrapers, modern transportation systems, and a vibrant cultural scene, while still preserving its historical essence through landmarks like the Forbidden City, Great Wall, and traditional hutongs.
