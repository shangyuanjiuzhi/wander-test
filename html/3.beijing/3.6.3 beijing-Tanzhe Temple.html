Tanzhe Temple Overview

A comprehensive guide to Tanzhe Temple, one of Beijing's oldest Buddhist temples.

Tanzhe Temple is one of the oldest Buddhist temples in Beijing, with a history dating back over 1,700 years. Built during the Jin Dynasty (265-316), it's located in Mentougou District at the foot of Tanzhe Mountain.

The temple is renowned for its ancient architecture, cultural relics, and peaceful mountain setting. It features numerous halls, pavilions, and courtyards, all surrounded by lush forests and clear streams.

The temple complex includes the famous Tanzhe Temple and Jieta Temple, both of which are important Buddhist sites with rich historical and cultural significance.

Recommended Tour Route

Tanzhe Temple is best explored by following a route that covers the main halls and courtyards. Start at the main entrance and proceed through the temple complex.

Key attractions include the Mahavira Hall, Hall of Heavenly Kings, and various Buddhist statues.

The temple is surrounded by lush forests and clear streams, making it a peaceful retreat from the city. Don't miss the opportunity to explore the surrounding mountain area, which offers hiking trails and scenic viewpoints.
