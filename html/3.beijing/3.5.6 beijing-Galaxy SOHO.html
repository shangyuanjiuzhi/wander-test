Galaxy SOHO Overview

A comprehensive guide to Galaxy SOHO, one of Beijing's most distinctive modern commercial complexes.

Galaxy SOHO is a modern commercial complex located in the Chaoyang District of Beijing. Designed by the late Iraqi-British architect Zaha Hadid, it opened in 2012 and has since become one of the city's most recognizable architectural landmarks.

The building's distinctive curvilinear design, characterized by flowing lines and rounded forms, creates a futuristic and dynamic appearance. It houses a mix of office spaces, retail stores, restaurants, and entertainment venues, making it a popular destination for both business and leisure.

Galaxy SOHO's innovative design and prime location have made it a popular spot for photography, especially among architecture enthusiasts and social media influencers. The complex's interior spaces, with their sweeping curves and natural light, are equally impressive.

Recommended Tour Route

Galaxy SOHO is best explored by following a route that covers main attractions and architectural highlights. Start at the main entrance and proceed through the building.

Key attractions include the main atrium with its impressive curved ceiling, the retail spaces on the lower levels, and the rooftop areas which offer panoramic views of the surrounding cityscape.

Don't miss the opportunity to photograph the building from different angles, both inside and outside. The complex's distinctive design makes it a popular subject for architectural photography, especially during golden hour and at night when it is illuminated.
